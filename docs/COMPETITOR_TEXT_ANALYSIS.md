# Competitor & Market Content Analysis — ILARIA Intimates

*Internal strategy doc. Mechanics/structure only — never copy competitor wording, never name a competitor (or other brand) in published ILARIA copy, captions, alt text, or hashtags.*

## Purpose, method & freshness

A working analysis of **what comfort-shaping intimates brands actually publish** on Instagram/TikTok, read through ILARIA's current direction (see `docs/CONTENT_TOPIC_SYSTEM.md` §0). It feeds the scored idea pool — we adapt *structures*, not words.

**Current direction (the lens).** ILARIA is a "beauty-portal" brand: women look and feel chic and gorgeous **at any weight, figure, and age**, shown through **styling and product features**, never slogans. Tone: good vibes, versatility, quality, softness, easy care, desire/aspiration.

**Retired — do NOT analyze for or build toward (tested and rejected):** age-targeting / "over 40" / midlife / perimenopause; "why your body changed"; purchase-anxiety / problem-led framing; body-fixing ("hide flaws", "transform", "flawless", "snatched", before/after); generic empowerment slogans.

**Method & freshness.** WebSearch + WebFetch across brand marketing pages, editorial trend guides, one Instagram reel, and 2026 platform-engagement benchmarks; adversarially verified (20 of 25 sampled claims confirmed). Compiled mid-2026. Tags: **[verified]** = survived verification; **[pattern]** = generalized; **[hard-data]** = measured (not marketing copy).

> **Evidence honesty (read this first).** The only *measured* engagement data here is **format-level** (carousel vs reel benchmarks). Every brand-specific finding comes from the brands' own marketing blogs or one sponsored reel — it documents **how brands frame content, not how many reactions a post earned.** Treat all brand findings as *framing patterns / test hypotheses*, not proven winners.

## 1. The one decisive, measured finding: format → goal

**[hard-data, verified]** On Instagram (Buffer 2026, 52M+ posts through Dec 2025; corroborated by Socialinsider/Metricool):
- **Carousels win engagement** — median **6.90%** vs single images 4.44% vs **reels 3.31%** (~109% more engagement per person reached than reels).
- **Reels win reach** — +36% vs carousels, +125% vs single images.
- Instagram "behaves like two platforms."

**ILARIA application:** **carousels** for save-worthy styling/feature/lookbook content (Territories 1–4); **reels** for top-of-funnel discovery (one-piece-many-looks, GRWM, comfort-in-motion). Put a *save* CTA on carousels. Revalidate quarterly — platform ranking shifts.

## 2. What competitors publish, by territory

### T1 — Styling & looks (the dominant high-signal territory)
- **One-piece-many-looks seasonal styling** **[verified]**. Benchmark: a "Styling a Bodysuit for Fall: 3 Easy Ways" reel — multiple outfits (blazer, layered looks) on one shaping piece. Notably published by a **third-party affiliate creator** (paid partnership), not the brand account → creator-led styling carries this format. *Adapt:* one ILARIA piece → 3 outfits; reel for discovery, carousel for saves.
- **Travel/packing capsule & interchangeable staples** **[verified, editorial]**. Fixed small capsule (e.g. 10 pieces) chosen so each item mixes-and-matches across destinations. *Adapt:* "pack-light capsule built on one base piece," carousel slide-per-piece.
- **The intimate as the enabler of a trend** **[verified, editorial]** — *the strongest age-agnostic styling hook.* 2026 summer trends explicitly hinge on the right base layer: tee-on-tee stacking needs "a form-fitting base layer that serves as a blank canvas"; the sheer-skirt trend "hinges on the right under-garment." *Adapt:* "what to wear under [the sheer skirt / slip dress / tee-on-tee]" — the piece that makes the look work.
- **Shaping piece as visible, season-styled outerwear, work-to-night** **[verified, brand blog]** — bodysuit under an oversized shirt (warm) / tailored coat (cold); "build your capsule" series. *Adapt:* seasonal styling carousels + a capsule series.

### T2 — Staying chic & self-care
- **Aspirational confidence-and-comfort framing, age-agnostic** **[verified, medium]**. Sampled brand styling content uses "feeling your best" / style-exploration language with **no age descriptors and no "your body changed" narrative** — directionally consistent with ILARIA's tested rejection of age framing. *Caveat:* the same copy still leans on appearance/"flawless base" product language → keep ILARIA's aspiration tied to **style and feel**, not to fixing the body.

### T3 — Product features & craft
- **Feature-led editorial = concrete spec + the benefit it unlocks + double-duty versatility** **[verified]**. Examples in-category: convertible crisscross-back straps, removable pads ("lifts and smooths without the squeeze"), "no bra required," "looks just as good solo or layered," seamless construction. *Adapt:* name the feature, then what it lets you do — for ILARIA's **wide/cushioned straps, massage pads, bodysuit inserts, detachable bottom** (these exact features have **no competitor benchmark** → net-new bets to test).

### T4 — Everyday wearability (the strongest age-agnostic benefit angle)
- **Invisibility + all-day ease framed as desire** **[verified]**. Recurring in-category framing: "forget you're wearing it," "no visible panty lines," seamless/sideseam-free panels that "virtually disappear," "invisible under your clothes," daily wear as desirable. *Adapt:* "works under anything / forget you have it on / no lines" — feature reels and benefit carousels. **Do not copy the verbatim lines** (they are competitor wording).

## 3. ILARIA playbook by territory (templates — adapt, don't copy)

All ILARIA-original, compliant. Hook lines are drafts to test; CTAs are hypotheses (see §4).

- **T1 Styling:** "One [piece], three outfits — same morning." · "Everything for a 3-day trip, and it all mixes." · "The [sheer-skirt / slip-dress] look only works with the right layer underneath — here's it." → carousel (saves) / reel (discovery).
- **T2 Staying chic:** "Getting ready when you actually like getting dressed." · a calm GRWM that ties the good mood to the styling, not to fixing anything → reel.
- **T3 Features:** "Bodysuit, but the bottom unsnaps." · "Wide straps, no shoulder dents by 3pm." · "Cushioned inside, smooth outside." → carousel/feature reel; always *feature + what it unlocks*.
- **T4 Wearability:** "On at 8, forgot about it by noon." · "No lines, even under the clingy dress." · "On in 10 seconds." → reel / benefit carousel.

## 4. CTA, hooks & hashtags

- **CTAs are unproven — treat as A/B hypotheses.** Prior research *refuted* comment-keyword→DM superiority, "DM shares = #1 signal," and loss-aversion hooks. The **one verified engagement lift is replying to comments** → "ask your styling/fit question, we answer each one" is the safest baseline; "save this look / save before you pack" is a saveable-framing hypothesis on carousels.
- **Hooks:** lead with **desire/showcase and specificity** (outcome, "what to wear under," feature aha), not problem/explanation. Retire delayed/generic openers and the old fixed motifs.
- **Hashtags [verified convention]:** "how-to-wear" + everyday-staple tags — e.g. #StyleTips, #WardrobeStaples, #EverydayStyle, #BodysuitOutfits, #OOTD, plus true feature tags (#WireFreeSupport-style). Accurate tags only; **never a competitor brand name.**

## 5. What we are NOT assuming (refuted / unproven)

- ❌ Competitors moved *away from* slimming/body-policing language (refuted ×2) — they run wearability/invisibility **and** slimming in parallel. ILARIA's anti-fixing stance is a **deliberate choice and differentiator**, not the category norm.
- ❌ "Wearability instead of slimming" outperforms (unproven) — A/B test.
- ❌ Mesh/sheer *dresses* as an underwear-layering trend (refuted); sheer *skirts* + base-layer styling is the verified one.
- ⚠️ Per-post engagement leaderboards for the named competitors were **not** obtainable — only format-level data is measured.
