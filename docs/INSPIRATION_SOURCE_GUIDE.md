# Inspiration Source Guide

Last updated: 2026-05-13

## Purpose

The Inspiration Inbox helps the planner reuse proven social mechanics without copying another brand's content.

Use it for:

- Competitor posts
- Pinterest references
- Instagram posts
- TikTok posts
- Internal ideas
- Saved creative examples

## Selection Criteria

Use an inspiration source when most of these are true:

- Product or audience overlap with the current project
- Similar platform behavior
- Similar buying objections or education needs
- Content format is realistic for the team to produce
- Performance looks meaningful relative to the source account
- The mechanic can be adapted without copying copy, claims, or design

## What To Capture

For each source post, capture:

- Source type
- Source name
- Platform
- Post URL
- Date
- Format: Reel/TikTok, carousel, static banner, product demo, UGC, founder/creator, review/proof, offer/promo
- Hook
- Visual structure
- Product or topic shown
- Offer used
- CTA
- Visible metrics
- Comment themes and objections
- Why it likely worked
- What the current project can adapt
- What must not be copied

## Signals To Discount

Discount or flag posts when:

- Performance looks paid-amplified without matching comments/saves
- Comments are mostly giveaways, spam, bots, or influencer-tagging loops
- The creative depends on celebrity, major PR, or very expensive production
- The hook relies on body shame, unrealistic transformation, or unsupported claims
- The product or audience is too different from the current project

## How This Feeds The Plan

The planner should reserve a portion of the selected period for inspiration-backed patterns, then fill the remaining slots with original strategic posts.

Recommended balance:

- Attraction: hooks, relatable situations, social-native microdrama
- Education: fit, use case, product detail, how-to-choose
- Trust: reviews, guarantees, returns/exchanges, real-life proof
- Desire: styling, product-on-body, color/product detail
- Conversion: offer, bundle, next action, comment-to-shop CTA

## Working Assumption

The source list should be reviewed after each planning cycle. If a source repeatedly produces low-relevance patterns or clearly distorted signals, remove it from active planning.
